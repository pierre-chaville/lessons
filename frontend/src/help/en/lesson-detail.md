# Lesson detail

The detail page centralizes editorial content, workflow tracking, and processing actions for a lesson.

## Displayed information

- Title, date, duration, and audio filename
- Full course path
- Linked themes
- Short summary (if present)
- Native audio player

## Workflow and pipeline

The right panel shows:

- Assigned editors
- Lesson status (based on your permissions)
- Status of each pipeline step
- The **Run tasks** button

A banner appears while processing is running.

## Available tabs

Tabs are shown dynamically when related data exists:

- Summary
- Edited version
- Sources
- Transcript
- Workflow audit log

## Common actions

- Edit summary, edited version, and transcript (with proper permissions)
- Export/import content (Markdown, Word, PDF depending on tab)
- Browse version history and restore older revisions
- Run remaining pipeline steps

## Hebrew terms

When the summary or the edited version was generated with Hebrew terms (native or partial Hebrew mode), each term found in the glossary is shown the way you chose with the **אב** button at the top of the page: transliterated, vocalized Hebrew or Hebrew without niqqud, optionally followed by the Hebrew, the transliteration or the translation in parentheses. Quotations can always stay in Hebrew.

Hover, tap or focus a term (dotted underline) to see all its forms: vocalized Hebrew, transliteration, translation and, for books and quotations, a link to Sefaria. Terms not yet in the glossary are shown as written.
