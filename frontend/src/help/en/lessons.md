# Lessons page

This page helps you browse lessons quickly and open lesson details.

## Quick flow

- Use the course tree on the left to scope the list to a branch.
- Use title search and facets (Hebrew year, status, themes).
- Adjust sorting depending on your task (date, name, status).
- Open any lesson card to access lesson details and workflow tools.

## Tip

You can combine multiple filters, then clear them one-by-one or all at once.
